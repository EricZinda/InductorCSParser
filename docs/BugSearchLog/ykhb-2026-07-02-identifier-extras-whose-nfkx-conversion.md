But `AddProjectedRunes` puts the converted runes of every non-form-stable entry into the start / body sets the compiled rule matches against. Same hole on the body side (`extraBodyRunes: TokenSet.Single(0x00A0)` made "a b" parse as one identifier) and the digit flavor (`extraStartRunes: TokenSet.Single(0x00B2)`, which converts to "2"). The whole 2026-06-03..06 fix series keyed its checks off "has a multi-rune conversion" while the set-rewrite path from 278bc23 has no such filter.
