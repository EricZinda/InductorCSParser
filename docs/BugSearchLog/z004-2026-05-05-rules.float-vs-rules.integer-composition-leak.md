- 2026-05-05: Rules.Float() vs Rules.Integer() composition (sign-leak)
    - Reviewed: every public factory in `Rules.cs` (`Token`, `Literal`, `LiteralIgnoreAsciiCase`, `OneOf`, `NoneOf`, `ScanWhile`, `ScanUntil` overloads, `AnyToken`, `Not`, `Peek`, `AllOf`, `FirstOf`, `BetweenInclusive`, `OneOrMore`, `ZeroOrMore`, `Optional`, `AtLeast`, `AtMost`, `Exactly`, `Eof`, `Integer`, `Float`, `InlineWhitespace`, `AnyWhitespace`, `EndOfLine`, `Identifier`, `WithinToken`). The audit was looking for cases where a composite factory wraps another public factory and silently inherits a clause the outer factory's documentation doesn't promise.
    - Categories: docstring-vs-grammar drift in composed factories, factory-of-factory inheriting optional prefixes / suffixes, divergence between what the factory matches and what the example output in the docs implies.
    - Found and fixed (fl0a): `Rules.Float()` was documented as "an optional leading -, one or more digits, a literal '.', and one or more digits" and explicitly said "Doesn't handle ... leading '+'." But the implementation was `AllOf(Optional(Token('-')...), Integer(), Token('.'), Integer())`, and `Integer()` itself carries `Optional(FirstOf(Token('+'), Token('-')))`. So the actual grammar was `-?[+-]?\d+\.[+-]?\d+`, not `-?\d+\.\d+`. `Float().Parse("--3.14")`, `Float().Parse("3.+14")`, and `Float().Parse("3.-14")` all succeeded.
    - The fix inlines `OneOrMore(OneOf(TokenSet.Digits))` for both the integer and fractional digit runs (so the inner Integer's sign doesn't leak into the fractional position) and replaces the leading-sign matcher with `Optional(OneOf("+-").Flatten(FlattenType.Flatten))` so a leading `+` is accepted symmetric with `Integer()`. The docstring is rewritten to match the new grammar: "[+-]?\d+\.\d+", at-most-one leading sign, no signs in the fractional part. Six regression tests in a new `FloatRuleTests.cs` lock in the three shapes that match (`3.14`, `-3.14`, `+3.14`) and the three classes that don't (double leading signs, mixed front signs, signs in the fractional part).
    - Other composed factories audited and clean: `AnyWhitespace()` reuses `EndOfLine()` and the CRLF-cluster handling is intentional (the doc explicitly calls out "CRLF as a unit"). `Identifier()` composes `WithinToken` over `OneOf(start)` / `OneOf(body)` and the optional clauses inside it are intentional and documented. `EndOfLine()` composes `Literal("\r\n") | OneOf(LineTerminators)` and the CRLF-first ordering is documented.
    - Also added the "Composed public factories silently inherit their inner factory's optional clauses" pattern bullet to `docs/PotentialBugSources/`.
